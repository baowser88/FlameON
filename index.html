Program Overview

Ultimate demands repeated max-effort accel/decel, cutting, and jumping over long points and games. With 2 lifts/week for ~35 weeks, this is a long linear-to-undulating block periodization: build tissue capacity and strength first, then convert it to rate-of-force-development (power), then sharpen reactive qualities as May approaches.

Plyometrics are handled entirely in your separate plyo sessions (2x/week) — this program is built to add zero extra jump/bound/landing volume on top of that.

Back extension has a fixed weekly slot in Day A every phase given your injury history — it's treated as non-negotiable, not phased out as the program progresses.

Every 4th week of each phase is a deload: cut sets ~40%, keep intensity. Supersets (A1/A2, B1/B2) mean no pause between the two exercises, rest only after the couplet — except contrast pairs in Phase 3-4, which rest briefly between the two (flagged where they appear).
